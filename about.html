The founder

Shiro Gichanja

Thirteen years in luxury client experience and professional training, and a career spent raising the standard of the industry she works in.

Shiro founded D'tails Aesthetics on a simple conviction: that a good result is never an accident. It comes from reading the skin properly, choosing the right treatment for it, and telling the client the truth about what will and will not work.

Alongside the clinic she is Principal and Franchise Partner at the Beauty Therapy Institute (BTI) Nairobi, where she trains the next generation of therapists, and founder of Aesthetics Spa Supplies, through which she helps beauty professionals and clinic owners across Africa source equipment and supplies from trusted global manufacturers.

She speaks regularly on the business of beauty — most recently on the panel at The Skin Summit — and is as invested in how the industry is run as in how it treats skin.

The clinic

D'tails Aesthetics

At D'tails Aesthetics we believe in results with a touch of luxury.

The clinic sits on the mezzanine floor at Gallant Mall on Parklands Road — a small, quiet suite rather than a busy salon floor. There is a consultation room with a skin analyser, treatment rooms for facials, brows and massage, and a reception you are welcome to sit in rather than queue at.

What happens there is skin-transforming facials, brow perfection including microblading and lamination, relaxing massage and expert waxing. Every single visit opens with a professional skin analysis, so treatments are tailored rather than assumed. Whether the concern is pigmentation, dullness, congestion, or simply needing an hour to yourself, the approach is the same: treat, pamper, and elevate the glow.

Tools are single-use. All blades, hand tools and wipes are disposed of as biohazardous waste after every appointment.
