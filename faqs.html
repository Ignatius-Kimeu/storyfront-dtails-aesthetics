Booking & payment

4 questions
Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes. A KES 1,000 deposit secures your appointment. It is fully redeemable and is deducted from your final bill, so it is not an extra charge — it simply holds the slot.

How much notice do you need to cancel or reschedule?

At least four hours. That lets the team manage the schedule and offer your slot to another client.

Do you take walk-ins?

Walk-ins are welcome. Booking ahead is encouraged though, so you are seen on time and with minimal waiting.

Which treatment should I book if I’m not sure?

Book a skin analysis. It is KES 1,500 and 30 minutes, and it is free inside every package. The therapist assesses your skin’s hydration, texture and condition, then recommends the treatments and products that suit it. Guessing is the expensive route.

Facials & skin

4 questions
How do I know which product is right for my skin?

Start by understanding your skin concerns. Get a consultation and a skin analysis done by a therapist. That will help you understand your skin better, and from it you get a recommendation of the products that best fit your skin.

Is extraction necessary during a facial?

Extraction removes build-up such as blackheads, whiteheads and excess oil trapped deep in your pores. At D'tails we do this gently and professionally, to clear congestion, prevent breakouts and let your skin breathe again. Think of it as a detox for your face.

What’s the aftercare for a facial?
  • Avoid direct sun for 24 hours, and always wear sunscreen
  • Do not touch the treated area for 24 hours
  • Use gentle moisturisers to keep the skin hydrated
  • Do not use retinol or other harsh treatments for 72 hours
  • Do not apply makeup for 24 hours
  • Book your next appointment

Above all: follow your therapist’s advice.

Why does every treatment start with a consultation?

Because D'tails offers several different facial treatments, and the right one depends on what your skin is actually doing. The consultation and analysis let the therapist understand your skin and the products you are already using, before anything is chosen.

Microblading & permanent makeup

7 questions
What is microblading?

Microblading is a semi-permanent brow tattoo performed with a small handheld tool, using a feather-stroke technique to produce the appearance of fuller eyebrows or to enhance the natural features of the face. It is a softer, more subtle cosmetic tattoo than some of the older methods.

Body tattoos use ink, while cosmetic tattoo procedures involve pigments. These pigments are designed to gently fade over time. Fading is desired, as it allows the artist to make changes to colour and shape over the years as the client’s natural colouring and facial features change.

Does it hurt?

We do all that we can to ensure the process is as comfortable as possible. Before the microblading begins a topical anaesthetic is applied to the area to minimise discomfort, followed by a liquid anaesthetic during the procedure. Some clients may feel a slight discomfort, but it is relatively pain free for most.

Please note that if you are a frequent smoker, or have your appointment during your menstrual cycle, the pain level could be intensified.

How long does it take?

1.5 to 2 hours, which includes the consultation, shaping, colour match and the microblading itself.

How long does microblading last?

The treatment is semi-permanent and will fade over time. Touch-ups are recommended every 1–2 years to maintain shape, colour and detail. How often depends on a number of factors:

  • Skin type — oily skin fades faster
  • Pigment colour used — lighter colours fade faster
  • Sun exposure — over time this causes fading and discolouration of the pigment
  • Use of daily anti-ageing products — for example tretinoin (Retin-A) and alpha hydroxy. These increase your cell turnover rate and will shorten the life of your cosmetic tattoo
Is the follow-up appointment included?

Yes. In order to ensure that the brows heal perfectly, a minimum of two treatments are usually needed, 6–10 weeks apart on average. This first touch-up is included, as it gives us the ability to correct any areas that possibly faded, or allows the client to make suggestions if they want adjustments to the shape or colour.

A touch-up taken more than eight weeks after the first appointment is charged at KES 8,000.

Can I get microblading while pregnant?

No. As a precaution to avoid any complications, we do not perform cosmetic tattooing on pregnant women.

What products and equipment do you use?

To ensure the highest quality of safety, all of our tools are one-time use only. All blades, hand tools, wipes and any other product used during the procedure are disposed of as biohazardous waste after each appointment, giving each client peace of mind.

Brow aftercare & healing

2 questions
What does the healing look like, day by day?

Day 1 — Eyebrows are approximately 20–25% darker than the expected healed result. All clients will have moderate swelling and redness after the procedure.

Days 2–4 — Swelling and redness subside. Micro-strokes appear darker, because a scab forms over the microbladed areas.

Days 5–7 — Shedding begins.

Days 7–10 — Shedding should be complete. Strokes will appear light, which is normal.

Weeks 2–6 — Strokes begin to darken by the day. True colour surfaces by the sixth week.

What should I do while they heal?

Let the scabs fall off naturally — if you touch them you risk ruining the microblading. Keep the area clean and gentle, and contact your artist if you notice anything unusual such as heavy scabbing or irritation. Your therapist will give you the full routine and any ointment advice at your appointment.
