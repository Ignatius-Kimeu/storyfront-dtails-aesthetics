Before you come

The booking policy.

A KES 1,000 deposit

A KES 1,000 deposit is required to secure your appointment. This amount is fully redeemable and will be deducted from your final bill.

Four hours’ notice

If you need to cancel or reschedule, kindly inform us at least four hours in advance. This allows us to manage our schedule and offer your slot to another client.

Walk-ins welcome

Walk-ins are welcome; however, we encourage booking to ensure timely service and minimal waiting.

Payment at the clinic

Payment is taken at the clinic. Nothing is charged through this website, and no card or M-Pesa details are collected here.
